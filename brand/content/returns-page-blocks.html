Not the right backpack? You can return it within 30 days of receiving it, without giving a reason.

How to return a backpack

  1. Tell us within 30 days of receiving your order. Email geral@rovakivo.com with your order number and the backpack you're returning. You can also use the cancellation form at the bottom of this page.
  2. Pack the backpack securely, with everything that came with it.
  3. Send it to Rua da Quinta do Jacinto 23 R/C D, 1300-492 Lisboa, Portugal, within 14 days of telling us. We recommend a tracked service, so you have proof that you sent it.

Return postage

You pay the cost of sending the backpack back to us. If the backpack is faulty or we sent you the wrong one, we pay instead.

Your refund

We refund the price of the backpack. If you return your whole order, we also refund the delivery charge you paid.

We send your refund within 14 days of you telling us, to the payment method you used. We may wait until we receive the backpack, or until you show us proof that you've sent it, whichever comes first.

Sale items can be returned and refunded in the same way.

Condition of returned backpacks

You can unpack the backpack and try it, as you would in a shop. If it has been used beyond that, for example taken on a trip, marked or damaged, we may reduce the refund by the loss in value.

Faulty or damaged backpacks

Every backpack has the legal guarantee of conformity. Under Portuguese law, it lasts 3 years from delivery, and it applies in addition to your 30-day return.

If your backpack is faulty or isn't as described, contact us at geral@rovakivo.com with your order number and photos of the problem. You can choose a repair or a replacement, free of charge, including postage. If neither is possible, you can have a price reduction or a refund.

If your parcel arrives damaged, please tell us as soon as you can and include photos of the parcel and the backpack.

Swapping for a different backpack

To swap for a different backpack, return the one you have for a refund and place a new order.
